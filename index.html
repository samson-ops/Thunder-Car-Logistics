FAQ

Car Shipping Questions, Answered

How much does it cost to ship a car to or from California?

Price depends on distance, vehicle size, open vs. enclosed transport and how soon you need pickup. Cross-country moves from Los Angeles or the Bay Area to the East Coast are the most common lane we run, and in-state moves are often same-week. Use the quote tool above for an exact, all-in price in seconds.

How long does shipping take?

In-state California moves usually take 1–3 days. California to Texas or the Pacific Northwest runs about 3–6 days; California to the East Coast is typically 7–10 days. We share an estimated delivery window as soon as your vehicle is dispatched.

Is my vehicle insured during transport?

Yes. Every carrier we dispatch must carry active cargo insurance, and we verify it before assignment. Coverage details are available before you book.

Open or enclosed — which should I choose?

Open transport is the most common and affordable option and is how most new cars reach California dealerships. Enclosed adds full protection from weather and road debris and is the right call for luxury, exotic, classic and show cars.

Do I have to pay a deposit up front?

Payment terms are confirmed at booking. There are no surprise charges at delivery — your all-in quote is your price.

Can you ship a car that doesn't run?

Yes. Tell us in the quote and we'll assign a carrier with a winch and the right equipment to load it safely.

Can you pick up from a port, auction or dealership?

Absolutely. We regularly pick up at the Ports of Los Angeles and Long Beach, Copart and IAAI yards, Manheim, and dealerships statewide. We'll handle gate passes and release paperwork with you.

Do I need to be there at pickup and delivery?

You or someone you designate (18+) should be present to sign the inspection report at both ends. Many customers hand off to a neighbor, family member or building manager.
