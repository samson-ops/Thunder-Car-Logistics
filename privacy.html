Privacy Policy

Last updated October 19, 2026

Thunder Car Logistics ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect when you use thundercarlogistics.com and request a quote, and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Quote details you enter: pickup and delivery ZIP codes, pickup timeframe, vehicle year/make/model, operability and trailer preference.
  • Contact details: your name, email address and phone number.
  • Technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages visited, collected through standard web logs and analytics.

How we use it

  • To prepare and deliver your quote and to arrange, dispatch and track your shipment.
  • To contact you by phone, email or SMS about your quote or shipment.
  • To improve our website and services.

SMS messaging

By checking the consent box on our quote form you agree to receive SMS messages from Thunder Car Logistics about your quote and shipment. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time and HELP for help. Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Sharing

We share shipment details with the licensed motor carrier assigned to move your vehicle, and with service providers that help us operate our business (such as our CRM and payment processors). We do not sell your personal information.

Your California privacy rights

California residents may request access to, deletion of, or correction of the personal information we hold about them under the CCPA/CPRA. To exercise these rights, contact us at info@thundercarlogistics.com. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

Contact

Questions about this policy: info@thundercarlogistics.com.
